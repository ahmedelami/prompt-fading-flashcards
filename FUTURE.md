## Select existing cover to group new covers with it
- Press G and click on an existing cover to select it as the active group
- Visual feedback: "Selected group [step number]"
- Any new covers drawn will be added to that selected group (same step number)
- Click the same cover again to deselect/unselect it
- Can switch between different covers/groups by clicking on them
- Drawing from outside a selected cover starts grouping with that step
- Allows retroactively adding covers to existing step groups
